Companies expanding across Zurich and Amsterdam often ask the same question twice, once per city, when one answer would do. Hiring an **AI software agency** or **AI consulting** partner for either market rests on the same fundamentals, with a handful of real differences worth knowing before you shortlist.

## What is identical in both markets

- **The scope should be one workflow, not a platform.** Any agency that opens with a platform pitch instead of asking about your specific process is selling the wrong thing in both cities.
- **GDPR is the baseline.** Both Swiss and Dutch companies expect a vendor who treats data residency and processor agreements as part of the build, not paperwork bolted on afterward.
- **A named builder, not just a seller.** In both markets, the agencies worth hiring will tell you who writes the code and prompts before you sign anything.

## What actually differs

| | Zurich | Amsterdam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical buyer | Private banking, insurance, pharma | Fintech, logistics, Series A/B scale-ups |
| Pace of decision-making | Deliberate, compliance-involved early | Fast, reference-checked before commitment |
| Extra regulation | Swiss FADP alongside GDPR | GDPR, actively enforced |
| Tone that lands | Precise, understated, data-residency-first | Direct, specific, low tolerance for polish over substance |

Neither market is harder to sell into, they reward different things. Zurich rewards patience and a clean data story. Amsterdam rewards speed and a founder who will get on a call.

## How to shortlist either way

1. Write a one-page scope before any sales call: the job, the systems it touches, the date you need a first version, and what is explicitly out of scope.
2. Ask who builds, not who presents.
3. Ask what you own if you do not continue past the first engagement.
4. Ask how the agency handles GDPR (and FADP, if Swiss) for this specific workflow, not as a policy slide.

Agencies that shrink your scope instead of expanding it have usually shipped before. Agencies that turn a one-page brief into a 30-slide proposal are selling process, in either city.
