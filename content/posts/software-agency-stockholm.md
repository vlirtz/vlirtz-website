Searching **software agency Stockholm** returns a mix of product studios, staff-augmentation shops, design firms, and a growing number of AI-labelled consultancies. The labels are less useful than the shape of the first project.

This is the filter we recommend to operators who do not want another vendor that “can do everything.”

## Decide what you are buying

There are four different purchases hiding under the same search:

| You need | Hire |
| --- | --- |
| Extra engineers in your process | Staff augmentation |
| A customer-facing product from zero | Product studio |
| A workflow, portal, or internal tool | Delivery-focused agency |
| An agent or AI-heavy workflow | AI software agency with delivery, not only slides |

If you need the last two, a generalist web agency will burn budget on visual design while the integration work stays last. If you need a brand site, do not hire an AI lab.

## Scope on one page

Before you take sales calls, write one page that names:

- the user (role, not “the business”)
- the job they cannot finish today
- the systems already in place
- the date you need a first usable version
- what you will not build in this phase

Agencies that rewrite that page into a 30-slide proposal are selling theatre. Agencies that come back with a smaller scope and a risk list are usually the ones who have shipped.

## How Stockholm agencies typically price

You will see three patterns:

- **Fixed price for a thin first version.** Best when the workflow is clear. Insist on written acceptance criteria.
- **Time and materials with a cap.** Best when you are still discovering the process. Review weekly or you will fund exploration forever.
- **Retainer for a “product team.”** Only useful after something is in production.

Be wary of large discovery phases that produce decks and no software. Discovery can be a week of interviews plus a technical spike. It does not need a quarter.

## Team shape beats slide logos

Ask who will actually type. A partner plus two juniors you never meet is a different product from a senior engineer who joins your Slack. For AI work, you also want someone who can say no to a model call when a deterministic script is enough.

At VLIRTZ the work is founder-led. That is a feature for early projects: you get the person who will still be there when the integration is ugly. It is not a 200-person factory. If you need that, say so in the first call.

## When “software agency” should mean “AI agency”

Choose a specialist if the core of the project is:

- an agent that takes multi-step actions in your tools
- scoring or researching accounts before sales outreach
- deciding where AI is allowed to act without a person

Choose a general software agency if you need a conventional portal, ecommerce, or a mobile app with no model in the critical path. We still build ordinary software when it is the path to the AI job. [See services](/services).

## A short shortlist process

1. Send the one-pager to three firms.
2. Ask each for a two-week first milestone, not a six-month roadmap.
3. Speak to the person who will build, not only sales.
4. Compare how they talk about data leaving the EU.
5. Pick the one that made the problem smaller.
